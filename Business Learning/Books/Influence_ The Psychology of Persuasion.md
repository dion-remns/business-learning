# Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion

## Author: Robert Cialdini

## Summary
Robert Cialdini explores the psychology of persuasion, identifying 6 key principles that influence human behavior: reciprocity, commitment and consistency, social proof, authority, liking, and scarcity.

## Key Takeaways
- **Reciprocity**: People feel obligated to give back when they receive something
- **Commitment and Consistency**: People tend to act in ways consistent with their previous commitments
- **Social Proof**: People look to others' behaviors to guide their own
- **Authority**: People tend to comply with those they perceive as authorities
- **Liking**: People are more likely to be persuaded by those they like
- **Scarcity**: People value things more when they are scarce or in limited supply
