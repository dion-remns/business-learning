# Predictable Revenue

## Author: Aaron Ross

## Summary
Predictable Revenue introduces a scalable sales process that helps companies generate consistent, predictable revenue. The key idea is to separate sales roles into SDRs (Sales Development Representatives) and AEs (Account Executives) to specialize in prospecting and closing.

## Key Takeaways
- **Sales Role Separation**: Split sales into SDRs (prospecting) and AEs (closing) for better efficiency
- **Outbound Prospecting**: Focus on targeted outreach to high-value prospects rather than random cold calls
- **Lead Qualification**: Use BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) or similar frameworks to qualify leads
- **Sales Process Documentation**: Create a repeatable process that can be scaled and taught to new team members

## How This Applies to REMNS AI
For your AI appointment setter:
1. Think about how your AI can act as an automated SDR, handling initial prospecting and lead qualification
2. Define clear lead qualification criteria for financial advisors
3. Create a repeatable sales process that your AI can execute consistently
4. Measure the effectiveness of your AI's outreach and adjust based on results
