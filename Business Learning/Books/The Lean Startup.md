# The Lean Startup

## Author: Eric Ries

## Summary
Eric Ries introduces the Lean Startup methodology, which applies lean manufacturing principles to startups. The core idea is to build products incrementally, test assumptions early, and pivot based on customer feedback.

## Key Takeaways
- **Build-Measure-Learn Feedback Loop**: Create a minimum viable product (MVP), measure its performance, and learn from customer feedback
- **Validated Learning**: Focus on learning that is validated by real customer data rather than assumptions
- **Pivot or Persevere**: When data shows your current strategy isn't working, be willing to pivot to a new approach
- **Innovation Accounting**: Use metrics to measure progress, set goals, and communicate results

## How This Applies to REMNS AI
For your AI appointment setter for financial advisors, the Lean Startup approach suggests:
1. Start with a simple MVP (e.g., basic cold calling AI with limited features)
2. Test with a small group of FAs to validate your value proposition
3. Measure key metrics like call success rate and appointment conversion
4. Pivot your offering based on feedback from early users
